# The Treaty of Cathedral

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 323 (Cathedral).

## The Treaty

After the Xyd pilgrims called on the **Taj Kehal** (their mother church), the Xyd sent armed solar-templar vessels to the Orb Radiant. A three-way struggle broke out — Xyd templars, GTA starhunters, and Fusion megacorps. When Fusion Star Force cruisers arrived, the GTA blinked: fearing a diplomatic incident with the Fusion, the GTA signed a treaty with the Taj Kehal **outlawing plasma-bird harvesting** in exchange for trade concessions.

## The Consequence

The treaty didn't end the harvest — it pushed it underground. GTA starhunters lost their licenses. Some left. Many stayed and took the Xyd's offer: paid mercenary work escorting fledgling plasma birds out of the cluster, or **privateering** — preying on Fusion harvest ships for the Xyd's bounty. The **Privateers of the Orb Radiant** were born.

## The Planet

The Xyd's sacred planet, **Xyd Vastaru** (translated "Cathedral"), became a wild open-port world: Xyd pilgrims, solar templars, GTA mercenaries and privateers, and merchants servicing them. The Fusion wanted it gone but the combined forces made it untouchable.

## Adventure Use

The Treaty is Cathedral's **moral skeleton** — the law is against harvesting, but everyone on the frontier is broke and the birds are priceless. Every skiff is a choice about which side of the Treaty you're on.
