# Plasma Birds

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 322–323 (Cathedral).

## What They Are

Plasma birds are **hyperspatial energy beings** — natural hyperspace engines that occasionally enter realspace to mate and spawn. They nest in the Orb Radiant's central suns, laying tens of thousands of "singularity eggs." Hundreds hatch every year.

## Why They Matter

Captured alive, a plasma bird is the catalyst for a **Chariot-class hypercore reactor** — the fastest FTL drive humans have. This single fact drives the entire Cathedral conflict: sacred to the Xyd, priceless to the GTA, harvest-worthy to the Fusion.

## Mechanics Hooks

- **Sacred vs harvested** — every plasma-bird encounter is a moral check (Xyd law, Treaty, black market).
- **Hypercore catalyst** — the signature skiff's hypercore references a captured bird (see `Mechanics/Plasma_Bird_Hypercore.md`).
- **Hatchings** — a nesting ground visit is a random-encounter table (hundreds hatch each year).

## Adventure Use

The plasma bird is Cathedral's **signature asset and its conscience**. The demo's two-doctrine skiff (GTA-regulated vs privateer) is literally the question of what you do with a captured bird.
