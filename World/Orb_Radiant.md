# Orb Radiant

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 322–323 (Cathedral).

## Overview

The Orb Radiant is a globular cluster — a dense sphere of **100,000 stars packed into a region only 50 light-years across**. The lack of heavy elements in the ancient Population II stars, plus gravitational interactions, means Earth-sized planets rarely form there. Its only non-stars are a few thousand mostly airless Moon-sized rogue planets in eccentric orbits.

For centuries it was ignored by humans — until the discovery that changed everything: the Orb Radiant is the **nesting ground of the plasma birds**.

## The Nesting Ground

Plasma birds (hyperspatial energy beings) enter realspace here to mate and spawn. Adults laid tens of thousands of "singularity eggs" in and around the central suns. For the next hundred years, hundreds hatch each year. Since plasma birds are natural hyperspace engines, they're usable as **catalysts for Chariot-class hypercore reactors** — if captured alive. That made the Orb Radiant the flashpoint of the galaxy.

## Flashpoint

GTA harvest ships, Fusion combines, freelance starhunters, and the Xyd pilgrims all collided here. The Xyd worshipped the birds as sacred; the humans saw reactors. Competition over the best spawning grounds turned violent. The **Treaty of Cathedral** outlawed GTA harvesting — which pushed many starhunters into the privateer trade.

## Adventure Use

The Orb Radiant is the **map and the stakes**. Every module here runs on the tension between the sacred nesting grounds and the scramble to harvest them. Outpost 9 is a neutral ring on the cluster's edge.
