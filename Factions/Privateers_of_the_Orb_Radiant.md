# The Privateers of the Orb Radiant

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 323 (Cathedral).

## Overview

The Treaty of Cathedral put GTA starhunters out of work — but rather than leave, many stayed and took the Xyd's offer. Some became paid mercenaries escorting fledgling plasma birds. Others turned **privateer**, preying on Fusion harvest ships and stations for the Xyd's lucrative bounty.

## The World

Cathedral itself became the privateer home port — a wild open-port world of Xyd pilgrims, solar templars, human mercenaries, privateers, and GTA merchants servicing them. A thorn in the Fusion's side too costly to remove. Not all GTA starhunters agreed with the law; some turned outright **star poacher and pirate**, feeding a large black market for captured plasma birds.

## Cast Hook

**Captain Rixia Talon** — Fusion privateer, dual plasma cutlasses, boarding anchor. **Tressa "Scraps" Vale** — independent broker/scrapper working out of the neutral freight hubs.
