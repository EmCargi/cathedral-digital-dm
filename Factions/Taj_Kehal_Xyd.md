# The Xyd & Taj Kehal

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 322–323 (Cathedral).

## Overview

The Xyd are an alien race that long followed the plasma birds' migration paths, which are occasionally disrupted by supernovae. They **worshipped plasma birds as sacred beings**, trapping only a few for ritual purposes. Xyd pilgrims regularly journeyed to the Orb Radiant to witness the hatchings and matings.

## The Taj Kehal

The Taj Kehal is the Xyd's **mother church**. When the GTA and Fusion harvesters began desecrating the nesting grounds, the Taj Kehal broke with tradition and sent its small number of armed **solar-templar** vessels to protect both their pilgrims and the hatching grounds. Xyd vessels began escorting fledgling birds into deep space where they could enter hyperspace and leave.

## Xyd Vastaru — "Cathedral"

The one Earth-like planet in 100,000 suns, named by humans after the Xyd's sacred planet. The Xyd welcomed the displaced GTA mercenaries and privateers — the combined force made the Fusion unable to eliminate the world. It became a wild, wooly open-port planet.

## Cast Hook

**Caelen Vhol** — a Human/Xyd Void Shepherd flying the Orb Radiant margins, faithful to the Taj Kehal's protection of the birds. **Vael-Xis** — a Grey emissary, non-aligned observer guarding the Cathedral gate nexus.
