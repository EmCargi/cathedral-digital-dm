# Galactic Trade Authority (GTA)

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 322 (Cathedral).

## Overview

The GTA oversees **thousands of worlds** and their relationships. Member worlds are semi-independent — democracies, dictatorships, monarchies — but all recognize GTA primacy in cross-border trade, defence, and law. It has no large space navy, but a large space militia of armed trading ships that can act in time of need.

## Covert Arm

The GTA runs an elite top-secret covert-ops division, the **Galactic Sanitation Department** — "cleaners" trained as troubleshooters.

## At the Orb Radiant

The GTA is the **lawful extraction machine** — harvest ships, licensed starhunters, and now (post-Treaty) a watching eye over the privateer trade. GTA inspectors at Outpost 9 are the customs gate. The signature skiff's "GTA Authorized" doctrine is the legal lane.

## Cast Hook

**Inspector Kaelen Drake** — GTA customs officer at Outpost 9, corporate law made flesh. Protocol is his shield; protocol blindness is his fault.
