# Captain Rixia Talon

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Fusion Privateers of the Orb Radiant
**Role:** Outlaw targeting GTA convoys; illicit boarding gear
**Age:** 35

## Appearance

Slim, duel-scarred, twin plasma cutlasses at her hips. Boarding anchor on her belt. Moves like someone who has taken a ship by the throat more than once.

## Personality

Grandstanding, reckless, loyal to crew. Fights the Fusion's harvest ships for the Xyd's bounty, but her methods are all corsair — boarding anchors, cutlass flurries, smokescreen vents. Wants the spectacle as much as the score.

## Background

A Fusion-born starhunter who refused the Treaty and turned privateer. Preys on GTA convoys and Fusion harvest ships alike, taking the Xyd's bounty for liberated plasma birds. Flagship's crew calls her the best captain in the cluster; the GTA calls her the most-wanted.

## Abilities

Force Field 2. Critical Strike, Two Weapons. Military 3, Adventuring 2. Dual Plasma Cutlasses.
