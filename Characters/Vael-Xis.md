# Vael-Xis

**Race:** Grey (space-faring — Ch.3 Template, PDF p.44 / printed p.42)
**Faction:** Non-Aligned — Grey emissary
**Role:** Observer guarding the Cathedral Gate nexus
**Age:** unknown (centuries)

## Appearance

Small, pale, large dark eyes, thin limbs. Grey robes with nothing stitched on them — non-aligned means neutral in every sense. Reads the beacon with a fingertip on the relay.

## Personality

Cold, precise, patient. Observes the gate nexus without taking a side — the Xyd are right that the birds are sacred, the GTA is right that the treaty is law, and he is right that the beacon is the gate's tuning fork. Severs emotional ties before they cost him.

## Background

A Grey emissary posted to the Cathedral Wayline Relay for longer than the Treaty. Guards the gate because the Grey have always guarded gates — non-aligned, unclaimed, untouchable. Knows more about the Cosmic Web than the humans who built the skiffs.

## Abilities

Resilient (Vacuum, Cold, Radiation), Telepathy 2 (racial). Mind Shield 2, Sixth Sense (Gates) 2. Defect: Fragile 1.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Cold Detachment — emotional engagement is a liability he prunes.
- **Sixth Guard:** Empathic Disconnect — faced with irrational emotional sacrifice → severs connection, flees into void.
- **Levers:** Neural Dampener / Gate Calibration / Stasis Probe
