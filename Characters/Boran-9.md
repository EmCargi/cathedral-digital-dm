# Boran-9

**Race:** Woolie (naturally strong — Ch.3 Template, PDF p.57 / printed p.55)
**Faction:** GTA Labor Guild — Indentured Conscript
**Role:** Heavy-freight loader; the brute-force rescue/defection beat
**Age:** 33

## Appearance

Immense, wool-pelted, collared. A shock collar rings his thick neck. Hands like cargo claws. Bows his head when the collar hums.

## Personality

Docile by conditioning, strong beyond measure. The Woolie's natural strength made him a freight beast; the Labor Guild's collar made him obedient. Wants out, but the collar overrides everything.

## Background

Born into the GTA Labor Guild's indentured lists, bred for heavy freight. The collar is the conditioning; the impulses are the override. Assigned to Outpost 9's cargo docks — and to the moral choice the party must make: free him (lose the loader) or use him (keep the muscle, carry the guilt).

## Abilities

Superstrength 3 (Lift 1 tonne), Tough 2 (racial). Brutal, Lethal Blow. Defects: Obligated (GTA Labor Guild) 2, Impaired Speech 1.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Restraint Conditioning — the collar made obedience reflexive.
- **Sixth Guard:** Shock Collar Override — disciplinary pulse fires → drops weapons, curls into brace posture.
- **Levers:** Freight Crush / Kinetic Heave / Desperate Bellow
