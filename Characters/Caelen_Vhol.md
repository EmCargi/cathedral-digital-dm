# Caelen Vhol

**Race:** Human / Xyd Shepherd
**Faction:** Taj Kehal — Void Shepherd
**Role:** Plasma-skiff navigator flying the Orb Radiant margins
**Age:** 26

## Appearance

Sturdy, weather-beaten, flight-scarred. Wears the Xyd shepherd's tunic with a pilgrim's charm over a space-suit lining. Hands never far from a thruster control.

## Personality

Faithful, steady, quiet. Believes the plasma birds are sacred — flew the Taj Kehal's escort lanes before the Treaty, watches the hatchings like a shepherd. Serves the gate nexus because he trusts Vael-Xis' read on it.

## Background

Born on a Xyd escort skiff, raised in the pilgrim lanes. Escorted fledgling plasma birds into deep space before the Fusion made it a war. Signed with the Taj Kehal's solar-templar support. Now flies the margins of the Orb Radiant — and knows the beacon is more than a navigation aid.

## Abilities

Spaceflight, telekinetic guidance of cargo and birds, steady hands on the stick. Spaceflight 2, Telekinesis 2. Lightning Reflexes, Steady Hand. Piloting (Spacecraft) 3, Navigation (Space) 2.
