# Tressa "Scraps" Vale

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Independent Broker — dockside scrapper
**Role:** Neutral fixer operating out of neutral freight hubs
**Age:** 31

## Appearance

Slight, grease-stained, sharp. Oversized salvage suit with more pockets than panels. Wields a torch-wrench like a wand.

## Personality

Wry, greedy, loyal to the salvage. Works every neutral freight hub in the Orb Radiant — buys low, smuggles sideways, sells to whoever's paying. Would sell the Treaty for scrap if it were worth the weight.

## Background

Burned a GTA convoy as a young starhunter's fixer, earned the "Scraps" name and a Wanted flag. Now runs Outpost 9's shadow economy — the scrapper who knows every ship's weak weld and every inspector's price.

## Abilities

Data Access, portable armoury, concealment. Mechanics (Spacecraft) 3, Burglary 2, Business 2. Defect: Wanted (GTA) 1.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Sunk Cost Greed — too invested in the salvage to walk away clean.
- **Sixth Guard:** Hull Eviction — airlock compromised → abandons salvage unconditionally to save the suit.
- **Levers:** Jury-Rig Bypass / Scrap Flare / Slipway Vent
