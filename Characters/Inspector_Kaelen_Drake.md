# Inspector Kaelen Drake

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Galactic Trade Authority — Outpost 9 customs
**Role:** Corporate law / customs marksman enforcing trade monopolies
**Age:** 40

## Appearance

Squared, uniform-creased, heavy tactical armor over GTA grey. Stun baton at the hip, heavy laser pistol holstered, manifest pad in hand. The uniform does the talking.

## Personality

Protocol-bound, exact, fair within the letter of the law. Anti-smuggler not out of cruelty but conviction — the Treaty is the law and the law is the Treaty. A bribe is a data point; a clean manifest is a good day.

## Background

Decades in the Galactic Sanitation Department before transferring to customs. Ran the Orb Radiant postings when the harvesters clashed with the Xyd. Signed the Treaty's enforcement with the Taj Kehal. Now the human gate the privateers must pass.

## Abilities

Heavy Tactical Armor (AR 10), Stun Baton, Heavy Laser Pistol. Police Sciences 3, Law 2, Street 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Protocol Blindness — the letter of the law blanks the spirit.
- **Sixth Guard:** Jurisdiction Collapse — counter-bribe or extralegal warrant flashed → paralyzed by protocol conflict.
- **Levers:** Regulatory Impound / Suppressive Volley / Riot Interlock
