# Cathedral Wayline Relay

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 323 (Cathedral — the planet is the wayline to Earth).

**Type:** Ancient pre-spaceflight navigational beacon attuned to the World Gate
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's lore node (`node_04_beacon_core`)

The beacon predates the humans who found it. It's a relay of the Cathedral gate nexus — attuned to the wayline that runs between Cathedral and Earth, the planet's low-orbit "haunted" space station gate, and the deeper Cosmic Web. Very few in this universe understand its significance.

The **Grey Emissary** (Vael-Xis) guards this nexus — a non-aligned observer who reads the beacon's calibration the way a tuning fork reads a chord.

**Mechanics:** A `lore` node with a `Sixth Sense (Gates)` reader — reach it, and the party learns the gate is real and the beacon is the key to reaching it.
